## Delivered slice and architecture

Lattice QM now selects either its unchanged harmonic path or a distinct quartic/double-well model. Four editable recipes (Gaussian limit, single-well quartic, symmetric and tilted double wells) supply drafts. The applied physical action and Metropolis controls remain in the sidebar; the periodic path is featured above six available optional scientific tools. The quartic path action and local difference live in pure [`quarticOscillator.ts`](../src/models/quarticOscillator.ts), the three-uniform transition in [`quarticOscillatorSampler.ts`](../src/algorithms/quarticOscillatorSampler.ts), and explicit model dispatch plus segment measurements in [`fieldRun.ts`](../src/engine/fieldRun.ts). A quartic segment has `reference=null`; no Gaussian mean/covariance is manufactured. A separate worker runs optional [`quarticTransfer.ts`](../src/models/quarticTransfer.ts) analysis only while its tool is mounted, with a four-result validated-parameter cache and stale-response cleanup. No archive schema or earlier sampler changed.

The finite path measure is `exp(−S_a)∏dx_j`, with `S_a=Σ[(x_(j+1)−x_j)²/(2a)+a(r x_j²/2+λ x_j⁴/4−h x_j)]`, `a=β/N`, and one periodic closing bond. The endpoint at β is drawn but not sampled twice. One local Metropolis attempt consumes site, proposal and decision uniforms; rejected configurations remain eligible for a scheduled retained measurement. Full-segment pair products, scalar moments, accepted/rejected counts and retained path-average sign counts belong to each immutable parameter segment. The latest 256 whole paths alone drive recent plots. The sign diagnostic counts changes only between retained paths and is not a physical tunneling rate. A model/N switch and Reset seed a fresh session; compatible physical changes preserve path and RNG in new measurement segments.

## Numerical evidence and status gates

The independent finite-spacing transfer kernel is the symmetric Trotter product, internally shifted by `Vmin`. A 49-node trapezoid grid on `[-L,L]`, 97 nodes on the same interval, and 121 nodes on `[-1.25L,1.25L]` compare spacing and cutoff independently. Leading eigenpair residuals and orthogonality are explicitly recomputed. A trusted approximate lowest gap requires ≤3% changes in both comparisons, ≤1e−8 residual/orthogonality, and a resolvable ratio (`aΔ>1e−8`); the shifted leading eigenvalue must not exceed 1 beyond tolerance. Failure yields under-resolved/unavailable status and no headline gap. The thermal transfer correlator uses the full numerical eigenbasis and is checked separately at zero, quarter-circle and midpoint lag. Successive-grid agreement is practical evidence, not a certified error bound.

## Limits and deferred work

The transfer results are approximate finite-spacing references, not exact continuum energies or certified interval bounds. Very small splittings, difficult coordinate cutoffs or insufficient higher-mode convergence can be under-resolved. A fixed warm-up budget, a handful of sign changes or a provisional sampled cosh indicator cannot establish equilibrium or a tunneling rate. No nonlinear uncertainty interval or automatic plateau fit is asserted. Spatial φ⁴, HMC, instanton extraction, automatic continuum campaigns and archive-schema changes remain outside M4c1.
