# 1D classical Ising chain — implementation review

## Scientific result

Lattice Systems now offers a distinct one-dimensional Ising chain alongside the original 2D square-lattice page. An open chain has 2–64 sites and `N−1` bonds; a periodic ring has 3–64 sites and `N` bonds, including one closing bond. The worker uses `lx=N, ly=1, periodicY=false, jy=0`. There is no vertical self-link or transverse physical observable. The original 2D indexed geometries, including their periodic length-two parallel bonds and exact 2×2 panel, retain their previous representation and tests. Physical `J,h,T` retain the M2 bounds; `S=H/T`, with no extra beta. The sampled energy density is physical `e=H/N`.

The existing random-site lazy Metropolis and heat-bath transitions supply local sampling without a second Monte Carlo engine or altered RNG draw order. One nominal sweep is `N` site updates with replacement. Same-chain parameter/sampler/schedule Apply preserves spins and RNG while opening a new warm-up and measurement segment; changed length/boundary and Reset start a seeded session. The chain's retained x-axis spatial products span the full current segment through `r≤16` (or the geometry's smaller natural limit), whereas 2D keeps its original four-displacement workload. The absent y bond-product summary has `count=0`, null statistics and an empty y spatial series. The archive validator requires these values for 1D only and still requires populated y moments for 2D. Existing v1/v2 portable data and 2D captures remain compatible; no archive schema changed. Chain CSV rows are explicitly labeled `1d-ising-chain`; saved records use a one-dimensional model label and parameter list rather than a fabricated transverse coupling.

[`isingChainTransfer.ts`](../src/models/isingChainTransfer.ts) contains the React-independent 2×2 calculation. Scaled positive matrix products yield finite-N periodic traces and open boundary-vector contractions without forming a large power of the leading eigenvalue. It returns log Z, site means, raw and site-centered connected pair correlations, physical mean energy, finite-volume susceptibility and the signed transfer spectrum. The bulk length is `ξ=−1/log|λ−/λ+|`; negative ratio means alternating correlations. Near-degenerate eigenvalues report unresolved rather than an unjustified length. A resolved `ξ>N` warns that the finite chain may lack an asymptotic decay regime. The ordinary nearest-neighbor 1D model has no finite-temperature phase transition. The [mathematical guide](math/ising-chain-transfer.md) derives the conventions and separates spatial transfer, Monte Carlo transition and Euclidean QM transfer operators.

## Presentation

The chain's default featured display is a selectable one-dimensional spin configuration. Above 17 sites it shows a local window around the selected site, with numeric access to every site; a periodic closing bond is drawn only when the whole ring is visible and otherwise described as outside the window. It marks the selected and latest updated sites. The ToolDeck initially opens exact-versus-sampled raw spatial correlation and magnetization history. The gallery also offers transfer matrix/eigenvalues, bulk length, exact finite observables, physical-energy history/histogram, recent-window autocorrelation/blocking, update outcomes and the actual local transition. Tool choices and site selection read worker snapshots without sampling. The 2D square canvas, 5×5 neighborhood and 2D Tc example do not appear on the chain page. Presets change draft settings until Apply.

## Validation

Independent enumeration checks open N=2,3 and periodic N=3,4,5 for log Z, mean m/e, susceptibility and every displayed raw and site-centered connected correlation under positive/negative coupling, nonzero field and high/low temperatures. The four-site ring agrees with the Small Systems graph action/reference after `K=J/T,b=h/T`. Closed zero-field open/ring formulas, negative subleading eigenvalue signs, J=0, and allowed N=63/64 extreme physical inputs are tested. Local flip costs match separate full-H differences for all small spin words. Seeded Step-versus-batch replay covers both samplers, snapshot cadence, warm-up/stride and parameter segments. Archive tests round-trip current and historical captures and reject invented transverse values or false origin counts.

The complete `npm run check` pipeline passed: Prettier, TypeScript, **381 unit tests in 50 files**, the `/lattice/` production build, and **283 passing browser tests** across desktop Chromium, a 360px phone viewport and mobile WebKit; two pre-existing sweep-budget browser cases remain skipped. Browser tests cover periodic N=32, open N=2, low-temperature ξ warnings, negative-J alternation, exact-versus-sampled plotting, ToolDeck exchange/replacement, Step, Run/Pause, Reset, segments, saved-record identity and no phone-width overflow. The existing 2D 2×2, neighborhood, five-workspace, Small Systems, QM, Fields, Experiments and archive tests also passed.

Limitations: very long bulk lengths cannot be resolved once the signed eigenvalue ratio is indistinguishable from magnitude one in floating point; no unsupported precise ξ is displayed then. The latest 1,000 measurement coordinates are plotted, while spatial means use all retained measurements in each surviving segment. Local-chain errors are correlated, so the spatial comparison deliberately has no independent-sample error bars. Physical low-end phone performance has not been measured; the 360px and mobile-WebKit checks establish layout and function, not a hardware benchmark. The existing production bundle-size advisory remains.
