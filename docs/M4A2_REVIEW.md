# M4a2 review — Fourier Gaussian fields and the mean-zero GFF

**Scope.** This feature branch extends the merged M4a1 Fields & Paths workspace. It adds a direct finite DFT, an exact periodic spectral reference, independent spectral draws and mode-power inspection. It adds no oscillator path, interacting field, HMC, gauge model, backend or field archive format.

**Measure and normalization.** An absent `measure` discriminator retains the M4a1 ordinary massive measure `∏dφ`, `r>0`; open and mixed boundaries continue to use dense Cholesky. Fully periodic chains and rectangles through 64 sites may use a spectral reference with `λ(k)=r+4κx sin²(πkx/Lx)+4κy sin²(πky/Ly)` (omit y for chains). The constrained model has `r=j=0`, positive stiffness in every active direction and `Σφ=0` with **induced Euclidean measure**, not a delta constraint against ambient product measure. Its covariance is `Q⁺`, with `QC=I−11ᵀ/N`, and `log Z=(N−1)log(2π)/2−Σk≠0 log λ(k)/2`. A two-site periodic chain yields `[[1,−1],[−1,1]]/(8κ)`.

**Architecture and RNG.** [`fieldFourier.ts`](../src/models/fieldFourier.ts) implements an elementary real/imaginary DFT with forward unscaled and inverse `1/N`; [`gaussianFieldSpectral.ts`](../src/models/gaussianFieldSpectral.ts) selects and sums supported modes without passing a singular matrix to Cholesky. [`spectralFieldSampler.ts`](../src/algorithms/spectralFieldSampler.ts) transforms N real site-order normal draws and divides conjugate-paired coefficients by `√λ`. It consumes exactly `2N` RNG uniforms per field, projects the massless zero mode and residual spatial mean, and shares the Step/Run transition path. The original uncached Box–Muller formula was moved verbatim into [`normalDraw.ts`](../src/algorithms/normalDraw.ts); dense direct and local sampler branch order and RNG consumption remain unchanged. `FieldRun` chooses a reference by sampler, isolates same-support parameter segments, and rejects incompatible support changes. The UI starts a new seeded session on a support or geometry change. There are still at most eight segments, 256 recent full vectors per segment and full online site/pair moments. M3 archives and sweeps are untouched.

**Spectrum and display.** [`sampledModePower`](../src/observables/fieldSpectrum.ts) derives `E[|F(φ−μ)(k)|²]/N` from a segment's pair products and site means, so vector eviction and plot frequency do not change it. The finite-volume exact value is `1/λ(k)` for supported modes; the constrained zero mode is marked removed, never infinite. The 1D chart and 2D momentum grid have numeric mode selection for touch and keyboard use. The fixed-scale field profile/heatmap remains unchanged. Constrained inspection labels ambient action terms and omits the invalid ordinary fixed-neighbor conditional. Mode-power samples have no uncertainty or autocorrelation correction.

**Numerical evidence.** Independent tiny-lattice tests cover DFT inverse, Parseval, conjugate symmetry and Nyquist reality; 1D two-site and anisotropic 2×2 parallel-bond eigenvalues; `Q` acting on Fourier modes; massive mean/covariance/log Z versus the existing dense reference; `1/(Nr)` spatial-average variance; mean-zero `QC=I−P₀`, `C1=0`, symmetry, nonnegative quadratic forms and augmented-Cholesky pseudoinverse; constrained sample sum and ensemble covariance, mode power and `(N−1)/2` mean action. Full-segment aggregate power agrees with a direct transform of all 300 generated vectors even after 44 vectors have been evicted. Seeded Step/batch replay, support-change rejection and old sampler tests pass. The DFT test has an independent phase-sum oracle; the pseudoinverse test constructs an independent nonsingular augmented matrix.

**Performance.** A Vitest host benchmark on an 8×8 periodic mean-zero field measured **2.7 ms** for reference construction and **3.6 ms** for 100 independent draws (Apple desktop host, warm test process, 2026-10-11). These are reproducible order-of-magnitude measurements, not a phone latency guarantee. Direct `O(N²)` transforms remain suitable at the supported 64-site bound; no FFT dependency or separable transform was needed. The production main chunk remains just above the existing 500 kB advisory (about **512.7 kB**); no code-splitting machinery was introduced solely to suppress it.

**Source readability.** Geometry, DFT, reference, sampler, moment projection, worker ownership and React selection are in separate modules. Source comments state normalization, conjugate reality, induced measure and projection. Tiny hand-checkable tests and the [math guide](math/gaussian-fourier.md) make the calculation followable without trusting the UI. [SOURCE_GUIDE.md](SOURCE_GUIDE.md) gives an ordered student path and the math guide gives four modification exercises.

**Browser review.** Production desktop Chromium, 360 px phone Chromium and mobile WebKit exercise both massive and constrained examples, support reset, mode selection, Step/Run and no horizontal overflow. Screenshots show the 12-draw mean-zero chain with a selected nonzero mode: [desktop](screenshots/m4a2-desktop.png), [phone](screenshots/m4a2-phone.png). The profile and spectrum remain readable at phone width; selecting a mode never samples.

**Validation.** `npm run check` passed: Prettier, TypeScript, **316 unit tests**, production build and **145 production browser tests** across desktop Chromium, phone Chromium and mobile WebKit (**2 existing browser skips**). The suite includes prior workspaces, M3 JSON/CSV imports and exports, sweeps, URL sharing and existing worker kernels. The M4a2 browser tests cover support reset, mode selection, Step/Run, 2D momentum grid and phone overflow.

**Limits.** Full periodicity is required for spectral sampling; open/mixed massive models retain the dense path. Constrained fields have no local sampler in M4a2. The spectrum uses finite-volume exact powers and raw full-segment sample powers without error bars. The supported shape bound remains 64 sites, and field runs are not yet part of the M3 experiment archive. Later oscillator, φ⁴ and HMC work remain separate milestones.
