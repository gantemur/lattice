# Interacting Euclidean oscillator

Use units ℏ = kB = m = 1. The continuum Hamiltonian is

`H=p²/2+V(x)`, with `V(x)=r x²/2+λ x⁴/4−h x`.

At inverse temperature β, the supported finite path has N independent real coordinates, spacing `a=β/N`, and periodic `x_N=x_0`. The closing point is a repeated coordinate, not another degree of freedom. The implemented action is

`S_a(x)=Σ_{j=0}^{N−1}[(x_{j+1}−x_j)²/(2a)+a V(x_j)]`,

and the normalized finite-dimensional target has density proportional to `exp(−S_a)∏_j dx_j`. There is no additional β factor. The constant path mode fluctuates. `quarticPathAction` and `quarticLocalDifference` in [`quarticOscillator.ts`](../../src/models/quarticOscillator.ts) implement the same convention; their four-site and full-action-difference oracles are in [`quarticOscillator.test.ts`](../../src/models/quarticOscillator.test.ts). The supported teaching range is N=4–64, β=2–16, r=−4–16, |h|≤2, and either λ=0 with r≥0.0625 or λ=0.05–4. These are numerical/UI bounds. λ>0 confines even when r<0. λ=0 with r≤0 would leave a nonnormalizable constant mode and is rejected.

The random-site [`quarticTransition`](../../src/algorithms/quarticOscillatorSampler.ts) selects one site, proposes `x'_j=x_j+w(2u−1)`, computes the _complete_ on-site plus two-bond change `ΔS`, and accepts with `α=exp(min(0,−ΔS))`. A rejected state is unchanged but can still enter a scheduled retained measurement. Exactly three seeded uniforms are consumed per attempt, including rejections. One nominal sweep is N attempts with replacement. The warm-up budget is not a mixing proof. Compatible physical-parameter changes retain path and RNG while starting a separate segment; changing N, switching the QM family, or Reset starts a seeded session. The worker path is [`fieldRun.ts`](../../src/engine/fieldRun.ts), tested in [`quarticRun.test.ts`](../../src/engine/quarticRun.test.ts).

For each retained path, `C_raw(ℓ)=N⁻¹Σ_j x_jx_{j+ℓ}`. Full-segment pair-product aggregates retain all measurements even after the latest 256 vectors are evicted. At h=0 the exact equilibrium mean is zero by reflection symmetry; raw C is the appropriate connected equilibrium quantity, even if one finite chain remains in a well. For h≠0, the displayed `C_conn=C_raw−〈x〉²_sample` is a plug-in estimate using the segment's translational ensemble mean, not individual-path demeaning. It can be biased by poor mixing. [`quarticCorrelator.ts`](../../src/observables/quarticCorrelator.ts) implements the projection and conservative cosh-indicator gate; its tests cover centering and cancellation.

The independent symmetric transfer kernel is

`T_a(x,y)=(2πa)^(−1/2) exp[−(x−y)²/(2a)−a(V(x)+V(y))/2]`.

It represents `exp(−aV/2)exp(−ap²/2)exp(−aV/2)`, rather than exact finite-a `exp(−aH)`. Positivity as an operator follows from this factorization and the positive kinetic semigroup; positive pointwise entries alone are insufficient. The quantum trace and raw product-Lebesgue path integral differ by `Z_raw=(2πa)^(N/2)Tr(T_a^N)`. The Monte Carlo update transition operator is a third, unrelated operator.

For double wells, `Vmin` and possibly the ground energy are negative, so the unshifted leading transfer eigenvalue may exceed one. [`quarticTransfer.ts`](../../src/models/quarticTransfer.ts) uses `Vs=V−Vmin≥0` internally, preserving ratios and the gap. It restores Vmin to absolute levels. A symmetric positive-weight trapezoid Nyström matrix `K_ij=√w_i Ts(x_i,x_j)√w_j` is solved for all eigenpairs. It computes relative eigenpair residuals and orthogonality, then compares 49 nodes on `[-L,L]`, 97 nodes on the same interval, and 121 nodes on `[-1.25L,1.25L]` at the refined spacing. L depends on stationary points, harmonic width and kinetic width. A well-separated gap requires both relative refinement changes ≤3%, residual/orthogonality ≤1e−8, and no near-degenerate ratio (`aΔ≤1e−8`). These are practical convergence gates, not rigorous error bounds. An unresolved gap is not reported as zero.

For leading eigenvalues `t0>t1`, `Δ01(a)=−log(t1/t0)/a`. In a symmetric double well this is a tunneling-related low splitting only when resolved; with tilt it is the lowest excitation gap. The finite-β thermal raw correlator follows the full-spectrum identity `C(ℓ)=Tr(T^(N−ℓ) X T^ℓ X)/Tr(T^N)`, using ratios to t0. Its connected version subtracts the thermal mean `Tr(T^N X)/Tr(T^N)` squared. Short-lag correlators depend on higher states, so their resolution is checked separately at zero, quarter-circle and midpoint lags. A dedicated worker computes quadrature only while the optional tool is open and does not touch Monte Carlo RNG. [`quarticTransfer.test.ts`](../../src/models/quarticTransfer.test.ts) checks a direct small-matrix trace, eigenpairs, both refinements, the analytic displaced-harmonic limit, a weak-quartic perturbative check and an independent finite-difference continuum Schrödinger comparison. The finite-a and continuum gaps are different at nonzero a.

The optional sampled local cosh indicator is `acosh[(C(ℓ−1)+C(ℓ+1))/(2C(ℓ))]/a`, with periodic reflection at the last stored lag. It appears only after 64 retained paths and only for a valid positive, non-cancellation-sensitive ratio. It is a provisional indicator, not an automatic lowest-gap fit or an estimate with a justified confidence interval. More β, smaller a or longer sampling may be needed; agreement at a few lags does not certify mixing or spectral accuracy.

For symmetric double wells the classical minima are `±√(−r/λ)` with barrier height `r²/(4λ)` above them. Neither this barrier nor a change in the sign of a retained path's _whole-path average_ is a quantum tunneling rate. The sign counts and changes diagnose possible **algorithmic metastability** at the chosen measurement cadence and miss intervening changes. The physical transfer splitting and Monte Carlo mixing are different questions. Exercises: compare Gaussian λ=0 with its analytic mean/gap, vary λ at fixed β/N, then compare a symmetric and tilted well; finally reduce a at fixed β and inspect the separate quadrature and finite-spacing changes.
