# Stage D3 — Pedagogical Small Systems catalog review

## Scope and identities

The Small Systems sidebar now offers a compact native, grouped named-example chooser. **Elementary probability** contains the 0D Gaussian, single Ising spin and binomial count. **Small Ising graphs** contains the dimer, three-site open chain, ordinary triangle, frustrated antiferromagnetic triangle, four-site periodic ring, star and open 2×2 square. **Continuous small systems** exposes the existing Gaussian pair, symmetric and tilted quartic wells, and four-site coupled Gaussian and quartic presets. **Advanced examples** retain the one-site scalar Gaussian, four-site open Ising chain, K4 and true periodic 2×2 torus. The periodic two-spin double-bond chain and duplicate one-site graph-Ising perspective remain valid internal numerical cases rather than visible catalog items. A custom graph is not advertised before Stage E.

`smallCatalog.ts` stores example names, short teaching descriptions and recipes. An example ID is presentation metadata, separate from the mathematical family (`Model.kind`) and concrete system (domain, parameters and sampler). It is never sent to a worker or included in archived scientific data or schema-v1 scalar links. `SmallSystemsApp` alone maps a chosen recipe onto an existing `changeSettings` or `initialize` command. A different family or topology starts a seeded session; changes on the same topology retain compatible numerical state and RNG while starting an isolated parameter segment. Ordinary edits display the chosen example as modified, or a loaded/legacy configuration as customized. Reset replays the actually applied settings and seed. No change to the five mounted workspaces, worker protocols, numerical kernels, archive versions or scalar URL schema was needed.

## Graph mathematics and references

The Ising-only `isingGraphPreset` extends the domain with stable `v0`… vertices and `e0`… bonds: the dimer has two sites/one edge, the open three-site chain has three/two, and the triangle has three/three. Deliberate drawing positions make the dimer horizontal, the chain aligned and the triangle recognizable. All three have no face: a graph cycle does not itself specify a plaquette. The seven legacy `graphPreset` IDs and their edges, orientations and faces remain unchanged. The advanced torus still has eight separately identified bonds and four oriented faces; zero coupling does not delete an edge.

All Ising examples use `S=−ΣₑKₑsₜsₕ−Σᵢbᵢsᵢ`, with each edge counted once and no further inverse-temperature factor. Ordinary small graphs start at `K=0.5`, `b=0`. The frustrated triangle starts at `K=−1`, `b=0` on the same topology as the positive triangle. On an odd cycle, three negative pair preferences cannot all be satisfied: six configurations leave one bond unsatisfied and minimize the action. This finite system does not undergo a thermodynamic phase transition. The model notes offer the small closed-form references and a comparison experiment.

Independent tests check `Z=4cosh K` and `〈s₀s₁〉=tanh K` for the dimer; `Z=8cosh²K` and `〈s₀s₂〉=tanh²K` for the chain; and `Z=2e^{3K}+6e^{−K}` for positive and negative triangle couplings. An independent Cartesian-state calculation also checks all actions, normalized probabilities, flip differences, conditional laws and detailed balance of both local kernels. The frustrated case has six lowest-action states and the independently calculated finite-temperature pair correlation and magnetization masses. Seeded direct, heat-bath and Metropolis sequences replay exactly. Existing four-spin transfer, parallel-edge, self-link and torus checks remain in the suite.

## Interface and compatibility

The featured Ising and real-scalar graphs, initial galleries, exact panels, local inspection and optional tools are reused. New geometry IDs appear honestly in the advanced graph parameter selector, so fields and couplings remain editable without an unsafe fallback to a different graph. Topology changes clear selected site/bond and graph-specific tool assignments. Same-topology triangle sign changes preserve the configuration and begin a new segment. The existing scalar presets are directly selectable in the catalog and remain available inside the collapsible legacy editor. The scalar sampler draft now follows *applied* sampler changes (not ordinary worker snapshot rerenders), so selecting a quartic recipe shows Metropolis and cannot later submit a stale direct/heat-bath choice. The reverse Gaussian selection restores the direct default. A one-site classical quartic double well is not labeled quantum tunneling; coupled quartic marginals remain sampled-only.
